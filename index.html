About

Engineering mind, commercial focus.

I'm a B2B sales and business development professional with a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering, and a career that spans both engineering solutions and enterprise software.

As Business Development Manager at DreamOnline Limited — a Japan-backed Bangladeshi software company — I manage a portfolio of 30+ enterprise and strategic accounts and lead international business development for custom software, SaaS, AI, IoT and digital transformation work.

My day-to-day spans client discovery, solution consulting, technical and commercial proposals, government tenders and contract negotiation, working closely with engineering and delivery teams so that what we sell is what we deliver.

I'm looking to grow into a senior business development and key account leadership role where I can win strategic accounts, build lasting partnerships and drive profitable growth.
